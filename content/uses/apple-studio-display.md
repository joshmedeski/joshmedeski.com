## Features

- 27-inch 5K Retina display (5120 × 2880) at 600 nits
- P3 wide color and True Tone
- 12 MP ultra-wide camera with Center Stage
- Studio-quality three-mic array and six-speaker system with Spatial Audio
- One Thunderbolt 3 port (96 W charging) and three USB-C ports
