## Specs

- Case: Phanteks Evolv Shift XT

- CPU: AMD Ryzen 7 5800X
- Cooler: NZXT AIO with Noctua Chromax Black fans
- Memory: 32 GB Corsair Vengeance DDR4 at 3200 MHz
- GPU: EVGA GeForce RTX 3070 Ti XC3
- Power supply: EVGA SuperNOVA 750 W
- Storage: 500 GB Western Digital NVMe boot drive plus a 1 TB NVMe
