## Features

- AMD APU with a Zen 2 CPU (4 cores, 8 threads) and 8 RDNA 2 compute units
- 7.4-inch HDR OLED display, 1280 × 800 at up to 90 Hz, 1,000 nits peak
- 1 TB NVMe SSD plus a microSD slot
- 50 Wh battery, 3 to 12 hours of gameplay
- Wi-Fi 6E
