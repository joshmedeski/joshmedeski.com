## Features

- Direct-drive motor with 33⅓, 45, and 78 RPM speeds
- USB output for digitizing records on a computer
- Switchable built-in phono preamp, so it works with or without a receiver
- AT-VM95E dual moving magnet cartridge
- Balanced S-shaped tonearm with hydraulic lift and anti-skate control
- Variable pitch control with quartz lock
