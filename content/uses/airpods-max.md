## Features

- Apple-designed 40 mm dynamic drivers
- Active Noise Cancellation and Transparency mode
- Personalized Spatial Audio with dynamic head tracking
- Digital Crown for volume, playback, and calls
- Aluminum ear cups, knit-mesh canopy, and memory-foam cushions
- Up to 20 hours of listening with ANC on
