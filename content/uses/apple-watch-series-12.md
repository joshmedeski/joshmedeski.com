## Features

- S11 chip
- Wide-angle Always-On Retina display, up to 2000 nits
- Background heart rate readings every 5 seconds and HRV as often as every 5 minutes
- Blood oxygen, ECG, and wrist temperature sensing
- Sleep score, plus sleep apnea and hypertension notifications
- Up to 24 hours of battery, 38 in Low Power Mode
