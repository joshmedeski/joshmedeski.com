## Features

- M1 chip with an 8-core CPU, 8-core GPU, and 16-core Neural Engine
- 12.9-inch Liquid Retina XDR mini-LED display, up to 1600 nits peak HDR
- ProMotion, P3 wide color, and True Tone
- 12 MP Wide and 10 MP Ultra Wide rear cameras
- 12 MP Ultra Wide front camera with Center Stage and Face ID
- Thunderbolt / USB 4 port
- Up to 10 hours of battery life
