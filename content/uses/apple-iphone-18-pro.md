## Features

- A20 Pro chip
- 6.3-inch Super Retina XDR display with ProMotion up to 120 Hz
- 48 MP Fusion Main, Ultra Wide, and 4x/8x Telephoto cameras
- 18 MP Center Stage front camera
- Aluminum unibody with Ceramic Shield 2 on the front
- 256 GB storage
- Up to 36 hours of video playback
