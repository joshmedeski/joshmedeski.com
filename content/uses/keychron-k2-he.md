## Features

- 75% layout
- Gateron double-rail magnetic Hall effect switches
- Rapid trigger and adjustable actuation point per key
- Tri-mode connectivity: wired, 2.4 GHz, and Bluetooth 5.2
- QMK-based firmware, remappable in Keychron Launcher
- RGB backlighting and wood side accents
