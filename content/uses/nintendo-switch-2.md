## Features

- 7.9-inch 1080p LCD with HDR10 and VRR up to 120 Hz
- Up to 4K at 60 fps when docked
- 256 GB storage plus microSD Express up to 2 TB
- Magnetic Joy-Con 2 controllers with mouse sensors and HD Rumble 2
- C button for GameChat
