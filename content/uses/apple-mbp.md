## Features

- 16-core CPU and 40-core GPU on the M4 Max chip
- 16.2-inch Liquid Retina XDR display with ProMotion up to 120 Hz
- Three Thunderbolt 5 ports, plus HDMI, an SDXC card slot, and MagSafe 3
- 12 MP Center Stage camera, studio-quality mics, and a six-speaker sound system
- All-day battery life
