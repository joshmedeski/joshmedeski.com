## Features

- M5 chip paired with the R1 chip for real-time sensor processing
- Micro-OLED displays with 23 million pixels, more than a 4K TV per eye
- Up to 120 Hz refresh rate
- Eye and hand tracking with high-resolution passthrough cameras
- Dual-driver audio pods with Personalized Spatial Audio
- External battery with up to 2.5 hours of general use
