A new Obsidian vault is an empty folder, which is the worst possible starting
point for a note-taking system. This one comes pre-structured around Tiago
Forte's PARA method, so there's an obvious place to put a thought the moment
you have it — and an obvious path for that thought to travel as it turns into
real work.

## Structure

- 🌄 **Days** — daily notes (`YYYY-MM-DD.md`) as the single capture point.
  Events, tasks, and ideas land here first.
- 🏔️ **Projects** — action-oriented work with a defined outcome, split into
  active commitments and someday-maybe ideas.
- 🏠 **Areas** — ongoing life domains that need attention rather than
  completion.
- 👥 **Relationships** — profiles for the people and organizations worth
  remembering, with their context and history.
- 🛠️ **Resources** — reference material organized by topic, including a
  folder for reusable prompts.
- 📥 **Inbox** — the unsorted zone, triaged into projects, areas, or the
  archive.
- 📜 **Archives** — finished projects and retired areas, kept without
  cluttering the active work.

## Get started

Clone the repo and open the folder as a vault in Obsidian, or copy the folder
structure into a vault you already have. Everything is plain markdown with no
required plugins.
